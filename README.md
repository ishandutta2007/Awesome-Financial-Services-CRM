## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Salesforce Agentforce Financial Services](https://www.salesforce.com/financial-services/)**  

  Formerly Financial Services Cloud, now Agentforce Financial Services. AI-powered platform unifying customer data from banking, wealth, and insurance systems. Features flexible hierarchies for household relationship mapping, Meeting Concierge for advisor-client lifecycle management (AI meeting prep, transcripts, follow-up drafts), prebuilt Complaint Management template, Digital Origination for lending workflows, and Data Cloud integration for cross-channel unification. Supports 360° client views with interaction tracking on Salesforce Calendar .



- **[Wealthbox](https://www.wealthbox.com/)**  

  Modern CRM for financial advisors with flexible deployment for broker-dealers. Features contact management with households and relationships, repeatable client workflows, activity history with timestamped interactions, and granular permissions for supervisory oversight. SOC 2 Type II certified with 150+ integrations and open API. Omniview Reporting (Premier/Enterprise) provides organization-wide visibility for compliance officers. LPL Financial two-way sync eliminates manual data entry .



- **[Redtail Technology](https://redtailtechnology.com/)**  

  #1 CRM for Financial Advisors by market share. Purpose-built for financial services with client and household records, automated workflows, calendar integration, and compliance tools. Features Redtail Speak for compliant text messaging, Redtail Imaging for document management, and automatic email archiving. SOC 2 Type II certified with 100+ integrations including Orion, Schwab, and eMoney. Pricing: Launch $39/user/month, Growth $59/user/month .



- **[Practifi](https://www.practifi.com/)**  

  AI-powered CRM built on Salesforce infrastructure for RIAs and wealth management firms. Features native AI including Smart Process Builder (plain-English workflow automation), Follow-up Assistant (meeting notes to tracked tasks), Smart Notes (AI meeting synthesis), and Smart Notes AI Notetaker. Offers pre-built workflows for account openings and annual reviews. Trusted by 10,000+ users. Research shows Practifi firms achieve 80% faster implementations than custom builds .



- **[Encompass Tamarac Advisor CRM](https://www.tamaracinc.com/)**  

  Web-based CRM built on Microsoft Dynamics for independent advisors. Features automated workflows (onboarding, account opening, beneficiary updates), business intelligence reporting including Firm Valuation Profile, and seamless integration with Advisor View and Advisor Rebalancing. Single place to maintain shared data across systems. Mobile apps for iPad and Windows tablets. Salesforce integration available for firms using both platforms .



- **[Broadridge Advisor](https://www.broadridge.com/)**  

  Integrated with best-of-breed CRMs including Salesforce, Redtail, and Ebix SmartOffice. Smart Advisor provides digital investor communication platform with document delivery tracking, regulatory and marketing delivery, and delivery preference management. Addresses POS Stage 3 regulations and CRM2 pre-sale disclosure. Scalable from stand-alone to enterprise-wide configuration .



- **[AdvisorEngine CRM](https://www.advisorengine.com/)**  

  Purpose-built CRM by advisors for advisors. Features powerful workflow engine, activity tracking, drag-and-drop dashboard widgets, and growth tools for prospect engagement. Tracks Interests and Favorites for client personalization. Dashboards turn data into visualizations. Pricing: $69/user/month with minimum three users, unlimited document storage included .



- **[NexJ Systems](https://www.nexj.com/)**  

  Enterprise CRM platform built specifically for financial services. Features relationship hierarchy management for household views, data integration with core banking and portfolio systems, workflow automation with event-driven triggers, and client lifecycle management from prospecting to retention. Supports complex multi-entity client structures. Interaction Summary rolls up notes to household level. Real-time data synchronization with API-first architecture .



- **[Creatio Financial Services](https://www.creatio.com/)**  

  Named a Strong Performer in The Forrester Wave™: CRM Software For Financial Services, Q1 2025. AI-native platform unifying AI, no-code, and vertical CRM. Features pre-built financial services workflows for banking, insurance, and investment management. Customers praise out-of-the-box capabilities, UI that flattens learning curves, and value for money. 59% YoY revenue growth in financial services sector .



- **[Tier1 Financial Solutions](https://www.tier1fin.com/)**  

  CRM and deal management platform for investment banking and capital markets. Tier1 Mobile Now app enables on-the-go client management with call reports, deal tracking, and relationship mapping. Features LSEG ownership and profile data integration, past and live deals tracking, and contact management .



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Ghostfolio](https://github.com/ghostfolio/ghostfolio)**  

  Open-source wealth management software (AGPL-3.0) built with Angular, NestJS, Prisma, and TypeScript. Track and analyze financial portfolios with multi-currency support, performance benchmarking, and asset allocation insights. Self-hostable for privacy-conscious individuals and advisors. Active community with 7,000+ GitHub stars .



- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**  

  Open-source CRM (AGPL-3.0) adaptable for financial services with client relationship management, workflow automation, and reporting. Can be extended with custom modules for financial planning, compliance tracking, and household management.



- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)**  

  Lightweight open-source CRM (AGPL-3.0) with customizable entities for financial client management. Features REST API for integration with portfolio systems and flexible relationship modeling.



- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)**  

  Modern open-source CRM (AGPL-3.0) with custom data models for financial services. Features GraphQL/REST APIs, workflow automation, and relationship tracking. Can model households, accounts, and financial goals.



- **[Krayin](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm)**  

  Laravel-based open-source CRM (MIT) with lead and pipeline management. Can be customized for financial advisory workflows including client onboarding and relationship tracking.



- **[Odoo](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open-source business suite (LGPL-3.0) with CRM, accounting, and invoicing modules. Can be configured for financial advisory practices with client management and billing.



- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open-source ERP (GPL-3.0) with CRM capabilities and financial accounting. Suitable for advisory firms needing integrated client and financial management.



- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core)**  

  Open-source constituent relationship management (AGPL-3.0) used by nonprofits and membership organizations. Can be adapted for financial services with donor and relationship tracking.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Monica](https://github.com/monicahq/monica)** — Personal relationship manager (AGPL-3.0) for tracking interactions and reminders.

- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)** — Open-source ERP/CRM (GPL-3.0) with contacts, opportunities, and invoicing.

- **[Vtiger CRM](https://github.com/vtiger-crm/vtigercrm)** — Open-source CRM (MPL-2.0) with sales force automation and customer portal.

- Community **n8n** workflows for financial CRM automation and compliance notifications.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Twenty** or **SuiteCRM** as the core CRM with **Ghostfolio** for portfolio tracking, **EspoCRM** for lightweight client management, and **PostgreSQL** for persistence — creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade financial services CRM platform.
